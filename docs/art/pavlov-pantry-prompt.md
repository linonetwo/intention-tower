# Pavlov pantry room

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production background for a 2D side-scrolling four-head-tall character game.
Primary request: a bright, welcoming historical physiology laboratory pantry adjoining a research room, painted in a polished Korean fantasy side-scrolling RPG style, warm cream plaster, honey-colored wood, mint ceramic storage jars, labeled-free food containers and scientific supplies on recessed wall shelves.
Composition: landscape 1536x1024, perfectly straight-on side elevation, no perspective vanishing point, vertical walls and horizontal floorboards, no tilted floor. The playable ground/contact line is a perfectly level horizontal seam at y=805 (approximately 78.6 percent of image height); below it show the front thickness and warm plank cross-section of the platform. No furniture or prop may protrude into the foreground walking lane. All cupboards and supplies are recessed against the back wall.
There is a low open doorway at the left edge connecting to the adjacent laboratory, and high clerestory windows with gentle daylight. Keep the upper right half mostly clear cream wall so the engine can draw an actual second-floor wooden platform and ladder over it; do NOT paint stairs, ladders, balconies or another floor into this background. The room is a believable continuous interior, not scenery outside the room.
Style: soft painterly cel shading, crisp gentle brown outlines, restrained cozy colors, same physical scale as a small classroom or laboratory. No people, animals, UI, text, signs, watermark, modern dashboard styling, isometric geometry, strong perspective, floating furniture, or diagonal ground. This is a finished game background, not a concept sheet.
```
